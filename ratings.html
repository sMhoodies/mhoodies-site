METHODOLOGY

PUBLISHED · UNCHANGED

Liquidity depth · 16.7%

Two halves, averaged. Slippage on a $10,000 sell — can a real position be exited. And liquidity against market cap — is the valuation backed. Measuring only the second flatters tokens nobody trades; only the first flatters size.

Community · 16.7%

Real holder count on a log scale to 50,000. Pools and burn addresses are excluded — a pool is not a community member. This is the only criterion that rewards size, and it also sets the ceiling below.

Concentration · 16.7%

The top 10 holders' share of supply held outside the pool, with the single largest shown alongside. The denominator is total supply minus the pool's own balance, read by direct contract call — measured against the explorer's first page of holders instead, this ratio converges to the same value for every token and says nothing.

Trading activity · 16.7%

24h volume against liquidity. Full marks between 0.3x and 2.5x daily turnover. Below that is a dead book; above it, churn that is hard to explain by organic demand alone.

Scale · 16.7%

Market cap on a log scale from $10K to $100M. Two tokens can post identical ratios and be nothing alike: absolute size is a form of resilience. Log-scaled so that a price rally moves the score by a few points, not a grade — this measures how established a token is, not its momentum.

Track record · 16.7%

Pool age on a log scale to one year. The one input nobody can buy, borrow or pump: a three-day-old token has proven nothing, whatever its ratios say. Taken from the pair creation timestamp on-chain.

The ceiling

The average is then capped by holder count against the tier table. A token cannot outrank its community size — being too small to have problems is not the same as being sound. Capped rows are marked.

Delisted pairs

When an aggregator drops a pair — which happens below a certain liquidity or activity level — price and liquidity are rebuilt from the pool's own balances on-chain. Those rows are marked ON-CHAIN. Volume cannot be reconstructed this way and is left out of the average rather than scored zero.

Missing inputs

An unavailable input is excluded from the average rather than scored zero, and the row is marked PARTIAL. A rating built on three of four measures is not the same object as a complete one, and says so.

Every figure on this page is read from Robinhood Chain and DEXScreener at page load, and nothing is stored. Click any address to check it on Blockscout — if a number here disagrees with the chain, the chain is right.
Ratings are mechanical and unsolicited. No token pays to appear here, and none can pay to leave.
